# Deeper-Market
An application made for you to connect your preferred LLM in order to find and relevant financial market data and information, then display it in an organized, easily understandable/readable and user-friendly UI. The application is designed to give you the most recent and relevant information based on your desired preferences and/or personal financial portfolio to keep you up-to-date on the state of the financial market and your investments using information and data gathered from well known and lesser known sources depending on your set preferences and LLM.
